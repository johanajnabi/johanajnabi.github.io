Piano

YouTube Instagram

Piano is where I return to form, pacing, and restraint. I am drawn to the way a performance is shaped by touch, silence, and the architecture of a phrase.

I share performances online as @jopianotes, with a mix of classical and contemporary pieces that reflect the same curiosity for structure and nuance that draws me to science.

  • Focused on interpretation, tone, and narrative pacing.
  • Performances shared across YouTube and Instagram.
  • A creative counterweight to experimental work and analysis.

Observation

Wildlife Photography

Follow @jopxls

Through wildlife photography under the handle @jopxls, I try to capture brief encounters shaped by light, distance, and stillness.

It is a practice grounded in patience. The image often depends less on control than on waiting well enough to notice what is already there.

  • Centered on timing, light, and atmosphere in natural spaces.
  • Built around observation rather than staging.
  • A way of practicing attention outside structured research settings.

What these practices sharpen

Attention

Both music and photography reward subtle differences. Small shifts in tempo, texture, framing, or light can change the whole experience.

Timing

A phrase lands because it is paced well. An image works because the moment is met at the right instant. Timing shapes both practices.

Patience

Not every result can be forced. These creative habits reinforce the patience and persistence that also matter in long research cycles.

Creative work and scientific work are not separate worlds for me. Each one trains how I see the other.
