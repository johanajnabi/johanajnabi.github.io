Research Overview

How cells interpret injury-associated mechanics

My work sits at the interface of mechanobiology, cell biology, and epigenetics, with a focus on how wounded skin converts physical cues into durable changes in cellular behavior.

At the Jamora laboratory, I study how actin-dependent mechanics, signaling pathways, and chromatin regulators coordinate epidermal cell-state transitions during tissue repair and regeneration. I am especially interested in questions where physical microenvironments meet nuclear decision-making.

Current Focus

  • Mechanotransduction during epidermal wound repair.
  • Actin-dependent control of nuclear signaling and chromatin regulators.
  • Skin regeneration, stemness, and disease-associated tissue-state changes.
