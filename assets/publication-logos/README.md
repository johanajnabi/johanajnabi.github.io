# Publication Logo Assets

These files are used as small source/publisher badges on the academic and publications pages. They are hosted locally to keep the site fast and compatible with a self-hosted Content Security Policy.

- `biorxiv-logo.png`: bioRxiv logo from Wikimedia Commons, originally sourced from biorxiv.org.
- `wiley-logo.svg`: Wiley logo from Wikimedia Commons, used for the Wiley-published Clinical & Translational Immunology card.
- `biomed-central-logo.svg`: BioMed Central logo from Wikimedia Commons, used for the Cell Communication and Signaling card.
- `cell-press-logo.png`: Cell Press logo from Wikimedia Commons, used for the Cell Reports card.
- `Discover-logo.svg`: Discover (Springer Nature journal series) logo, used for the Discover Plants entry.
- `Frontiers-logo.svg`: Frontiers logo, used for the Frontiers in Immunology entry.
- `JID-logo.svg`: Journal of Investigative Dermatology (JID) masthead logo, used for the JID entry.

These marks are used only for nominative identification of publication venues or publishers. Trademark rights may still apply.
